      • Niva Bupa hikes premiums 8-9% annually to keep pace with medical inflation: CFO Vishwanath Mahendra

        Niva Bupa Health Insurance is prioritising profitable growth even as medical inflation continues to push up the cost of health cover, with the insurer raising premiums by around 8-9% annually and expecting this trend to continue. CFO Vishwanath Mahendra said smaller annual increases are preferable to sharp hikes every few years, while the insurer targets a 98-99% combined insurance service ratio by FY29 and mid-to-high teens ROE.
