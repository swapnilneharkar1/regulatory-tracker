      • ARCIL seeing fastest growth in NBFC originated retail bad loans: CEO Phanindranath Kakarla

        Asset Reconstruction Company India Ltd (ARCIL) is seeing the fastest growth in retail assets originated by NBFCs, even as bank originated, corporate and other retail portfolios continue to grow, with CEO Phanindranath Kakarla and CFO Pramod Kumar Gupta saying retail and SME assets are likely to gain a larger share of the portfolio as they grow faster than corporate assets. The company, which is set to become the first standalone asset reconstruction company to list in India, is also open to acquisitions of smaller ARCs.
