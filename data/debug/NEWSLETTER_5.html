Blackstone-backed Epsilon Bidco exits EPL for Rs 2,032 crore; HDFC Life buys Rs 74 crore worth shares in SBFC Finance

&nbsp;

Promoter Epsilon Bidco, owned by affiliates of the Blackstone Group, has exited EPL (earlier known as Essel Propack), the laminated plastic tubes manufacturer, for Rs 2,032 crore through open-market transactions on September 1.

EPL shares opened sharply lower following the deal and ended the session at Rs 240.35, down 8.28 percent, amid heavy volumes on the National Stock Exchange.

According to the bulk deals data published by the exchange, Epsilon Bidco Pte sold all its 8.44 crore equity shares, representing a 26.38 percent stake in EPL.

The shares were sold at Rs 240.57 apiece, valuing the stake at Rs 2,032.33 crore.

However, 3.37 crore equity shares, representing a 10.5 percent stake, of the total 8.44 crore shares sold by the Blackstone Group entity were bought by eight institutional investors.

Quant Mutual Fund emerged as the biggest buyer among them, picking up an additional 1.43 crore equity shares, equivalent to a 4.48 percent stake in EPL, for Rs 345.07 crore.

Sandeep Tandon-founded Quant Group, through Quant Small Cap Fund, already held a 2.47 percent stake in EPL as of June 2026.

Bandhan Mutual Fund also bought an additional 20.82 lakh shares, or a 0.65 percent stake in EPL, for Rs 50 crore at a price of Rs 240.1 per share. This was in addition to the 3.33 percent stake it held as of June 2026.

Further, Public Sector Pension Investment, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund, Edelweiss Mutual Fund, Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance Company, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, and 360 ONE acquired the remaining 1.72 crore shares in EPL for Rs 414.3 crore at a price of Rs 240.1 per share.

BNP Paribas sells Rs 37.4 crore worth of shares in Happiest Minds
BNP Paribas Financial Markets sold 10.19 lakh shares, representing a 0.66 percent stake, in digital engineering company Happiest Minds Technologies for Rs 37.42 crore at a price of Rs 367.23 per share.

The stock price crashed 10.87 percent to Rs 362.7, extending its downtrend for the fourth consecutive session, particularly after news of the company’s merger with ITC Infotech.


TIMF Holdings buys 0.58% stake in Symbiotec Pharmalab

TIMF Holdings, owned by US-based investment firm Think Investments, acquired 3.77 lakh shares, representing a 0.58 percent stake in Symbiotec Pharmalab, for Rs 37.3 crore at a price of Rs 989.2 per share.

Symbiotec Pharmalab shares rallied 11.1 percent to Rs 1,097.7 on the NSE, against the issue price of Rs 988 per share, on the listing day.

Nexus Global Opportunities Fund picks up additional 1.11% stake in Wonder Electricals
Nexus Global Opportunities Fund picked up an additional 15 lakh shares, representing a 1.11 percent stake, in Wonder Electricals for Rs 12.12 crore at a price of Rs 80.84 per share.

Nexus Global already held a 2.34 percent stake in Wonder Electricals as of June 2026.

HDFC Life Insurance buys 0.65% stake in SBFC Finance
HDFC Life Insurance Company bought 73 lakh shares, representing a 0.65 percent stake, in non-banking finance company SBFC Finance for Rs 73.73 crore at a price of Rs 101 per share.

Promoter acquires Rs 98.34 crore worth of shares in Hatsun Agro Product
Promoter Chandramogan R G acquired 8.25 lakh shares, representing a 0.37 percent stake, in dairy products company Hatsun Agro Product from Ravi Kirti Shah for Rs 98.34 crore at a price of Rs 1,192 per share.

Prudent Equity Ace Fund buys 0.93% stake in Manaksia Steels
Prudent Equity Ace Fund, owned by asset management firm Prudent Equity, purchased 6.15 lakh shares, representing a 0.93 percent stake, in flat and long metal products maker Manaksia Steels for Rs 6.38 crore at a price of Rs 103.83 per share.

Manaksia Steels shares rose 4.6 percent to Rs 104.1 amid heavy volumes on the NSE.
