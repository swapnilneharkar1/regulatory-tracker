Rupee opens 6 paise higher at 95.81 against the dollar as Brent crude eases

The rupee opened six paise higher on September 21, as Brent crude prices pared back some of its gains over the weekend, trading just above $100 per barrel, while traders stayed on the sidelines for more cues on geopolitical developments.

The local currency was trading at Rs 95.81 per dollar, as compared to RS 95.87 per dollar in the previous trading session. The rupee had briefly touched the Rs 96 per dollar mark last week before the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) swooped in to prevent any sharp fall in the currency.

Brent crude prices were trading 2 percent lower, at around $101 per barrel, after touching as high as $110 per barrel last week, as hope of diplomacy seemed to have gotten a chance after United States President Donald Trump expressed interest in meeting his Iranian counterpart in a meeting later this week.

Even then, tensions in the Middle East have not died down as both countries continue to launch attacks on each other.

Back home, market participants are expecting the RBI to intervene in the spot and forwards market, should there be any sharp depreciation in the currency.

“RBI does not seem to be in a mood to allow the Rs 96.00 mark to be breached while Brent oil prices are lower… markets are focused on US-Iran diplomatic efforts even as fresh attacks by Houthis kept tensions elevated,” analysts from Finrex Treasury Advisors, said.
