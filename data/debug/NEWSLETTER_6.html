      • FCNR(B) inflows set to hit USD 100 billion, nearly 3X 2013 levels; 76% of mobilisation is fresh money

        Indian banks have mobilised USD 73 billion through FCNR(B) deposits, ECBs and overseas foreign currency borrowings since June 8, with inflows accelerating to USD 16 billion in just eight days through August 21. Jefferies now expects total mobilisation to reach USD 90-100 billion by August 31, significantly above its earlier USD 70-80 billion estimate. Around 76 per cent of FCNR(B) mobilisation represents fresh money, suggesting the scheme is attracting new foreign currency into the banking system rather than merely replacing maturing deposits.
