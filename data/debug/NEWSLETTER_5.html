Iran expels Swedish diplomat in tit-for-tat move after Stockholm orders Iranian official out

Iran on Thursday ordered a Swedish diplomat to leave within 48 hours, a day after Sweden expelled an Iranian embassy official from Stockholm in a reciprocal move that deepened tensions between the two countries.

Iran's Foreign Ministry described Sweden's decision as “unjustified” and said it had summoned the Swedish ambassador in Tehran to protest the move.

The latest action followed Sweden's decision to order the Iranian embassy official to leave, citing activities it said were incompatible with the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.

Why Sweden Expelled Iranian Official

Sweden's Foreign Ministry said on Wednesday that it had ordered an Iranian embassy official to leave after determining that the person's activities were not consistent with the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.

The ministry did not specify the activities involved.

Swedish Justice Minister Gunnar Strömmer later said the decision was linked to what Stockholm described as Iranian activities threatening security in Sweden, including targeting people who oppose the Iranian government as well as Jewish and Israeli interests.

Swedish Security Service spokesperson Jonathan Svensson said Iran continued to use proxies, including criminal networks, to carry out attacks or acts of violence in Sweden.

Sweden's security service had previously accused Iran of recruiting criminal gang members to target Israeli and other interests in the country. Tehran has denied those accusations.

Iran Retaliates

Iran's Foreign Ministry called Sweden's expulsion decision “unjustified” and rejected the allegations against its embassy official.

Iran summoned the Swedish ambassador in Tehran to formally protest Sweden's decision before ordering a Swedish diplomat to leave within 48 hours.

Tehran also criticised what it described as Sweden's hostile rhetoric and accused Stockholm of supporting groups and individuals opposed to the Iranian government.

No further details were immediately provided by either government about the specific activities that led to the diplomatic expulsions.

Iran-Sweden Relations Have Been Strained

Diplomatic relations between Iran and Sweden have been tense for several years, with disputes involving security allegations and Swedish-Iranian citizens held in Iran.

Sweden has a sizeable Iranian community, including opponents of the Iranian government living in exile.

In March, Iran executed a Swedish citizen on espionage charges, according to Sweden's foreign minister.

Swedish authorities have also previously accused Iran of recruiting criminal networks to carry out attacks against Israeli and other interests in Sweden. Tehran has denied those allegations.
