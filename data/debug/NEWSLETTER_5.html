'Let this be clear and final warning': Prithvi Shaw hits back at Yograj Singh over lifestyle claims, warns of legal action

Prithvi Shaw has hit back at former cricketer Yograj Singh over his remarks about the batter's career downfall and personal life, warning that he could pursue legal action against individuals or platforms attempting to tarnish his reputation.

Yograj had recently spoken about Shaw's career struggles, blaming his alleged lifestyle choices for his failure to fulfil his potential. He also compared the Mumbai batter's situation with that of former India cricketer Vinod Kambli, who experienced a similar decline despite possessing considerable talent.

Speaking on the Sports Launchpad podcast, hosted by Saurav Yaduvanshi, Yograj discussed how off-field distractions can affect the careers of promising young cricketers.

"Why did Prithvi Shaw go downhill? It happened with Vinod Kambli before him. It was because of parties, womanising, cigarettes, and drinking. I once asked Kambli, ‘What are you, son?’ You have so much talent," Yograj said.

Recalling an incident involving Kambli, Yograj claimed that the former India batter's lifestyle had affected his performances on the field. He subsequently linked the same concerns to Shaw's career.

"You are coming to your room at 2.30 in the night. He said, ‘Your days are over, and we’re playing a different kind of cricket now.’ The next morning, Courtney Walsh bowled him, and India lost the match. Then the girl he was with left him, and no one offered him cigarettes and alcohol. The same happened with Shaw. When you go to parties, womanise, drink, and are in bad company, the same will happen to you as happened to them," he added.

Shaw, however, has strongly objected to the comments, making it clear that he is unwilling to accept what he considers deliberate attempts to malign his character. While acknowledging his past, the right-handed batter stressed that personal criticism should not cross the line into spreading misleading or defamatory claims.

"My past is a part of my life, and I have absolutely nothing to hide or be ashamed of. However, I will not tolerate individuals deliberately using my name, my personal life, or my past to spread negativity, create unnecessary controversy, damage my reputation, or gain publicity and attention at my expense. There is a clear difference between expressing an opinion and deliberately targeting someone with false, misleading, defamatory, or malicious content. Let this be a clear and final warning: Any individual or platform engaging in such activities may face appropriate legal action," Shaw said.

The India batter also indicated that he would not hesitate to take legal recourse to safeguard his personal dignity and public image. He maintained that choosing not to respond to every criticism should not be interpreted as an inability to defend himself.

"I reserve the right to pursue all legal remedies available to protect my dignity, privacy, and reputation. My silence should never be mistaken for weakness. I choose to move forward with dignity, but I will not hesitate to stand up for myself," he added.

Shaw's latest response comes after he had previously spoken about a difficult phase in his career. Last year, the batter admitted that he had not given cricket the attention it deserved during that period. His latest statement, however, draws a distinction between accepting responsibility for his past mistakes and allowing others to make allegations about his personal life that he believes are intended to damage his reputation.
